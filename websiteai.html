About the Event

Prompt Engineering 2026 is a college-level technical event designed to introduce students to the concepts, techniques, and applications of prompt engineering.

Participants will learn how to communicate effectively with generative AI systems, create structured prompts, improve AI responses, and explore real-world applications of large language models.

Event Highlights

Prompt Engineering Workshop

Learn the fundamentals and advanced techniques for designing effective prompts for generative AI tools.

Live AI Demonstrations

Experience practical demonstrations of generative AI applications and prompt optimization techniques.

Prompt Challenge

Compete with other participants by creating innovative prompts to solve interesting AI-based challenges.

Expert Interaction

Interact with industry professionals and researchers working in artificial intelligence and generative AI.
